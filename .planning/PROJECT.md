# Saanidhya Sharma's Arcade Portfolio

## What This Is

An insanely creative and futuristic personal portfolio website for Saanidhya Sharma, a Full Stack Developer, AI Engineer, and Creative Technologist. The site is an immersive digital experience built with a retro arcade and cyberpunk aesthetic, feeling like a playable cyberpunk terminal mixed with modern "Apple-level" smoothness.

## Core Value

Delivering a "WOW" experience through immersive, arcade-inspired UI/UX that instantly communicates Saanidhya's technical depth and creativity in AI and Full-Stack development.

- **Performance**: Must maintain high FPS despite advanced 3D effects and animations.
- **Design**: Dark mode only, neon/glow effects, pixel-mixed glassmorphism.
- **Mobile**: Touch-optimized 3D effects and responsive animations are required.
